## Updated provisional program now available

The provisional program has been updated to reflect schedule requests and other feedback received from presenters.

Several presentation slots became available following cancellations, allowing some contributions that had previously been assigned to the poster session to return to oral presentations.

Please see the [Program page](/program/) for the current schedule.

## Registration remains open

General registration for TNSAA 2026 remains open until **November 15, 2026**.

The regular contribution submission period closed on **October 15, 2026**, and presentation assignments are now listed in the program.

The banquet / networking reception payment deadline is also **November 15, 2026**.

Please visit the [Registration page](/registration/) for details.

## Poster session / networking reception / banquet

The poster session, networking reception, and banquet will be held on **December 16, 18:00–20:00** at **Kobe Portopia Hotel, Main Building B1F, Ikuta**.

Poster size and other poster specifications will be announced separately.

## Website launched

The official website of TNSAA 2026 is online.

## Venue confirmed

TNSAA 2026 will be held at the RIKEN Center for Computational Science (R-CCS), Kobe, Japan.
